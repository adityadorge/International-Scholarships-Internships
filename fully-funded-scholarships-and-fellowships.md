# Fully Funded Scholarships & Fellowships

### Global & Multi-Country Programs

* **[Rhodes Scholarship](https://www.rhodeshouse.ox.ac.uk/scholarships/applications/india/eligibilitycriteriamet/)**
* **Target Degree:** Postgraduate (Master’s / PhD) at the University of Oxford
* **Coverage:** Fully funded (Tuition, stipend, travel, visa)


* **[Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters Scholarship](https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters)**
* **Target Degree:** Master's
* **Deadline:** October – January (varies by program)
* **Coverage:** Full tuition, monthly allowance, travel, and installation costs


* **[Fulbright Foreign Student Program](https://foreign.fulbrightonline.org/about/foreign-student-program)**
* **Target Degree:** Master’s / PhD in the United States
* **Coverage:** Full tuition, living stipend, airfare, health insurance


* **[ADB-Japan Scholarship Program](https://www.adb.org/work-with-us/careers/japan-scholarship-program)**
* **Target Degree:** Postgraduate studies in economics, management, science, and technology across Asia-Pacific institutions
* **Coverage:** Full tuition, housing, books, medical insurance, travel allowance


* **[POSCO Global Scholarship](https://www.postf.org/en/asia/purpose)**
* **Target Degree:** Undergraduate / Graduate studies in Asian partner universities
* **Coverage:** Full tuition and living support

### United Kingdom

* **[Gates Cambridge Scholarship](https://www.gatescambridge.org/programme/the-scholarship/)**
* **Target Degree:** Postgraduate (Master’s / PhD) at the University of Cambridge
* **Coverage:** Full cost of studying, maintenance allowance, travel, family allowances


* **[Chevening Scholarship](https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/)**
* **Target Degree:** One-year Master's degree at any UK university
* **Coverage:** Full tuition, monthly stipend, travel, visa costs


* **[Commonwealth Scholarships](https://www.britishcouncil.in/study-uk/scholarships/commonwealth-scholarships)**
* **Target Degree:** Master’s / PhD at participating UK universities
* **Coverage:** Airfare, tuition fees, monthly living allowance


* **[Inlaks Scholarship](https://inlaksfoundation.org/opportunities/scholarship/)**
* **Target Degree:** Postgraduate study in top UK, European, and US institutions
* **Coverage:** Up to **$120,000 USD** covering tuition, living costs, travel, visa, and health insurance
* **Eligibility Criteria:**
* Indian passport holder residing in India aged **30 or under**
* Recognized Indian undergraduate degree with a minimum score of:
* **65% / 6.8 CGPA** (Humanities, Social Sciences, Law)
* **70% / 7.2 CGPA** (Sciences, Environment-related fields)


* Requires prior proof of admission to the target program/institution


* **Note:** Computer Science programs are currently excluded from this award.
